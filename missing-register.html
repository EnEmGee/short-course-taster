The missing register

Setting the scene ...

The voice clinic exuded nasal consonants and tired vowels. They said it was just another standard case: another vocalisation gone sideways.

I’d heard that before.

That’s why they call me in – when the voices don’t line up with the stories.

They hand me a folder. CAPE‑Vr. New form. Cleaner lines. Clearer labels. Better stimuli. Sharper calls on pitch, loudness, resonance – and a fresh lookout for nasality. More room for the details that matter: inconsistencies, instabilities, the quirks that give a voice an alibi ...

... or break it.

I don’t chase shadows. I track patterns. Recording by recording, note by note, I listen for what doesn’t belong.

Because every voice leaves a trace. And in this world, the truth isn’t what you say – it’s how you say it.
