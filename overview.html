Overview

About this course

This is a simulated experience of an ACU Short Course. It will show some of the features of a full course, which may help you decide if this is the learning experience for you. A full course is a mix of text, video, interactive objects, images and readings. ACU Short Courses run between 1 and 20 hours and are fully asynchronous so you aren't locked in to a schedule. Some of our courses lead to accredited learning at Australian Catholic University.

Time commitment

This short course should take you around 10 minutes to complete. People learn at different rates. The important thing is that you absorb each new piece of information before you move to the next.

What you will learn

By the end of this course, you'll be able to:

  • Navigate the features of an ACU Short Course, including interactive activities, reflection tasks and digital badges.

  • Explain how fat, sugar and salt each affect the flavour, texture and structure of food, and why changing them takes more than a simple swap.

  • Evaluate a diet trend by asking what is behind it: evidence, marketing, culture or something else.

  • Describe how the mission of the Order of Malta has changed from its origins in Jerusalem to its humanitarian role today.

  • Apply cognitive load theory to recognise when a task is overloading working memory, and how to design learning that avoids it.
