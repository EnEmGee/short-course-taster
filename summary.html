ACU Short Course digital badges

What are Parchment Badges?

Parchment Badges (formerly known as Canvas Credentials) is a digital badging platform that integrates with Canvas. It uses verifiable digital badges that allow you to display your professional achievement. A badge is a portable, shareable digital asset you can take with you when you go. Add the unique URL to your social media account(s), your digital resume, or your professional organisation's portal.

Signing up to Parchment Badges

The first time you want to access your Parchment Badges, you will need to create an account using the email you used for the course you just completed.

How do I share a badge?

You can download the badge or certificate as an image but the power of a digital badge comes from the metadata that sits behind it. People or organisations who want to check your credentials can look at the metadata and verify their authenticity.

Visit How do I share my earned badges from my Parchment Digital Badges Backpack? to learn more.
